Chapter 2: Most profitable regions

The barplot is going to help us with understanding, which regions are most interested in it, if there are any obvious profit leaders, and also find patterns.

Click to see the code

The first thing I am going to do is, obviously, creating a SQL query, which is going to give me the 15 most profitable states.

Input:

query="""

SELECT o.State, SUM(d.Profit) AS Profit

FROM Orders o

INNER JOIN Details d ON o."Order ID"=d."Order ID"

GROUP BY o.State

ORDER BY SUM(d.Profit) DESC

LIMIT 15 """

downfall="""
